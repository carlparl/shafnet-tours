# Shafnet credibility catalogue checklist

The website now supports verified company credentials, real team profiles and
source-backed customer reviews. These records are intentionally hidden until
they are complete and activated in Django Admin.

Do not add invented licence numbers, memberships, team identities or reviews.

## 1. Company credentials

Create one record for each current credential:

- Exact credential or registration name
- Issuing organisation
- Public licence, registration or membership number
- Short factual description
- Independent public verification URL
- Expiry or renewal date, when applicable
- Official logo, used only with permission

The website automatically hides a credential after its `valid_until` date.
Leave **Active** off until the record and verification link have been checked.

## 2. Team profiles

Collect the following from each person:

- Full public name
- Current role at Shafnet
- A concise factual biography
- Relevant qualifications that can be supported
- Languages spoken
- Approved professional photograph
- Written permission to publish the profile and photograph

Leave **Active** off until the person approves the final profile.

## 3. Verified reviews

For each review, retain:

- Reviewer’s public display name
- Reviewer location, when they published it
- Exact trip name
- Travel month or date, if known
- Rating from 1 to 5
- Review text that Shafnet has permission to republish
- Source platform
- Direct public URL to the original review

Only enable **Verified** after checking the direct source. Only verified,
active reviews with both a source name and source URL appear publicly.

## 4. Tour pricing and images

Before replacing “Price on request”, confirm:

- Currency
- Per-person or per-group basis
- Minimum number of travellers
- Accommodation category
- Resident or non-resident basis
- Included permits and activities
- Seasonal validity
- Single-room supplement
- Child policy

Each tour should also receive a licensed or Shafnet-owned landscape image of at
least 1600 × 1000 pixels. Confirm the image location before describing it as a
specific Ugandan destination.
