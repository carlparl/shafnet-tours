Who We Are

Shafnet Tours & Travels Ltd is a proudly Ugandan company based in Kampala. We specialize in both domestic getaways and international safaris across Uganda’s most beautiful destinations.

Whether you're looking for a relaxing weekend on the Ssese Islands or an unforgettable gorilla trek in Bwindi, we handle every detail with care, professionalism, and local expertise.
