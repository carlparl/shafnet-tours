Who we are

Proudly Ugandan. Personally attentive.

Shafnet Tours & Travel Ltd is a Kampala-based travel company creating local getaways and safari experiences across Uganda. Since 2018, our focus has been simple: understand each traveller, plan the details carefully and make the journey feel effortless.

Whether you are considering a quiet weekend on the Ssese Islands, a wildlife safari or a gorilla-trekking experience in Bwindi, we help shape a trip that suits your time, interests and pace.
