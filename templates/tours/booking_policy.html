Booking & Cancellation Policy

How requests become confirmed bookings and how quote-specific deposits, changes, cancellations and refunds are handled.

Effective: 15 July 2026
On this page Booking requests Quote-specific terms Confirmation and payment Customer changes Customer cancellation Changes by Shafnet Refunds Insurance and concerns
No hidden universal percentage

Supplier conditions differ between gorilla permits, accommodation, transport and other activities. Your final quote will clearly state the exact deposit, payment deadline and cancellation terms before you confirm.

01

Booking requests

Submitting a website form, email or WhatsApp enquiry does not create a confirmed booking and does not require payment. We will review availability and may ask for additional information before providing a final itinerary and quote.

A request remains subject to availability until Shafnet confirms it in writing and all stated confirmation requirements are completed.

02

Quote-specific terms

Every final quote will identify:

  • The services included and excluded.
  • The final price and currency.
  • Any deposit and the deadline for paying it.
  • The balance amount and payment deadline.
  • The cancellation, amendment and refund conditions for that itinerary.
  • Any supplier item that becomes non-refundable or non-transferable after reservation.

Those written quote-specific conditions form part of the confirmed booking and control where they are more specific than this general policy, subject always to applicable law.

03

Confirmation and payment

Your booking becomes confirmed only after you accept the final proposal, Shafnet confirms availability in writing, and any required deposit or confirmation amount has cleared.

Payment must use the method and account details stated in Shafnet's verified communication. Contact us using the telephone number or email published on this website if payment instructions unexpectedly change.

04

Changes requested by the customer

Ask for date, traveller, accommodation, route or activity changes in writing as early as possible. We will check availability and explain any price difference, supplier fee or non-refundable cost before making the change.

A requested change is not effective until Shafnet confirms it in writing. Some permits, tickets and supplier reservations cannot be transferred or amended.

05

Cancellation by the customer

Send a cancellation request in writing to shafnettours@gmail.com or through an agreed written communication channel. The cancellation takes effect when Shafnet receives and acknowledges it.

The amount recoverable will follow the cancellation conditions accepted with your final quote. It may reflect non-refundable permits, accommodation, transport, activity reservations, administrative work already completed and other supplier charges that were disclosed before confirmation.

We will provide a written calculation showing how the applicable outcome was determined.

06

Changes or cancellation by Shafnet

Safety, weather, park rules, road conditions, supplier availability and events outside reasonable control may require a change. We will communicate material changes promptly and take reasonable steps to provide a suitable alternative.

If Shafnet cancels a confirmed service and no suitable alternative is accepted, we will explain the refund or credit available after considering amounts recoverable from suppliers and any remedy required by applicable law. This paragraph does not remove rights that cannot legally be limited.

07

Refund process, missed services and delays

Where a refund is due, we will confirm the amount and expected processing period in writing after receiving the necessary supplier responses. Refunds are normally returned through the original payment route where practical. Bank, card, foreign-exchange or intermediary charges may affect the final amount unless applicable law requires otherwise.

Unused services caused by late arrival, no-show, voluntary early departure or failure to meet communicated travel requirements may be non-refundable where suppliers have already reserved or delivered them. We will still help identify any amount that can reasonably be recovered.

08

Travel insurance and concerns

We strongly recommend suitable travel insurance covering cancellation, interruption, medical needs, evacuation, baggage and the activities in your itinerary. Insurance is not a substitute for reading the quote-specific terms.

Raise any concern promptly so we have a fair opportunity to investigate and assist. Nothing in this policy prevents you from using a lawful complaint, regulatory or court process.
