Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect and share personal information when you enquire about or book a Shafnet journey.

Effective: 15 July 2026
On this page Who we are Information we collect How we use information Who receives information Retention and security Your rights Cookies Contact us
Plain-language summary

We use the information you give us to answer enquiries, plan requested trips, manage bookings and send service messages. We do not sell personal information.

01

Who we are

Shafnet Tours & Travel Ltd is a tour and travel company based in Kampala, Uganda. For information submitted through this website and our booking services, Shafnet determines why and how that information is used.

Questions about this policy or your personal information can be sent to shafnettours@gmail.com or raised by telephone at +256 789 472229.

02

Information we collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • The tour, intended travel date, number of travellers and trip preferences you submit.
  • Messages, enquiries and communication records.
  • Booking status, itinerary and payment-related records after you accept a quote.
  • Limited technical information required for website security, sessions and reliable delivery.

Please do not submit passport copies, medical information or payment-card details through the general booking form. If such information becomes necessary, our team will explain the appropriate secure method and purpose.

03

How and why we use information

We process information to:

  • Respond to an enquiry or booking request.
  • Check availability and prepare a tailored itinerary or quote.
  • Confirm, administer and support an accepted booking.
  • Send essential booking, status, safety and service communications.
  • Maintain business, accounting and dispute-resolution records.
  • Protect the website, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We use information where you have requested steps toward a booking, where it is necessary to provide an accepted service, where the law requires it, where a legitimate operational need does not override your rights, or where you have given consent. You may withdraw consent for consent-based use, but that does not invalidate earlier lawful processing.

04

Who may receive information

We disclose only what is reasonably necessary to deliver or support the requested service. Recipients may include:

  • Hotels, lodges, guides, transport providers, activity operators and permit authorities involved in your itinerary.
  • Website hosting, database, security and communication providers acting for Shafnet.
  • Our transactional email provider, currently Brevo, for booking and status messages.
  • Professional advisers, regulators or lawful authorities where disclosure is required or reasonably necessary.

Some service providers may process information outside Uganda. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate providers and safeguards consistent with applicable data-protection requirements.

05

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry or booking, customer support, accounting, regulatory obligations, fraud prevention and possible disputes. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including controlled access, secure hosting features and protected account credentials. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through open message fields.

06

Your rights

Subject to the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and other applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information and provide access to it.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete information where there is no lawful reason to keep it.
  • Stop or object to particular processing where the law gives that right.
  • Explain important decisions made using your information.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. If you remain dissatisfied after contacting us, you may raise a complaint with Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office.

07

Cookies and website sessions

The website uses essential security and session technologies needed to protect forms and display a booking confirmation. These functions are necessary for the service to work. We do not currently use advertising cookies through this website.

08

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal responsibilities change. Material changes will be shown on this page with a revised effective date.
